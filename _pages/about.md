Hello! I am a master's student in Computational & Applied Mathematics at the [University of Chicago](https://www.uchicago.edu/), where I work with Prof. Cong Ma on imitation learning.

I am broadly interested in reinforcement learning and its applications, imitation learning, sequential decision-making, and flow matching. Before UChicago, I studied mathematics at IIT Bombay and spent two years at FinMechanics working on derivatives pricing and risk systems.

Outside work, I enjoy chess, tennis, and learning Pokémon TCG.
