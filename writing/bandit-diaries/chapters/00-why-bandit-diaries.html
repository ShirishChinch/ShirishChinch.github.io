Hi! These are my notes on Bandit Algorithms by Tor Lattimore and Csaba Szepesvári. I’m planning to cover roughly one chapter every day, understand the main idea behind it, and share what I learn here. Last quarter, I took a course on reinforcement learning taught by Prof. Frederic Koehler. We learned about contextual bandits and algorithms such as LinUCB, SquareCB, and exponentially weighted forecasters. I found the course really interesting, and reading this book felt like a natural next step. The book studies a much wider range of bandit environments, the algorithms designed for them, and the ideas behind their analysis. When I first started reading it, though, I kept getting bogged down in the rigor and notation. So instead of trying to reproduce the book as formal lecture notes, I want to write about the main ideas as simply as possible: what problem each chapter is solving, why the algorithm works, and what the clever part is.
