Shirish Chinchanikar · A living series

This is where I work through bandit theory one chapter at a time.

The goal is not to compress the book into formal notes. I want to understand what each chapter is really saying, why the result matters, and where the main idea comes from—then write it down in language I would have found useful on a first read.

How this works

I read a new chapter, sit with the difficult parts, and try to explain the core lesson without hiding behind notation.
